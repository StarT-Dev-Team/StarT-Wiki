It seems this world operates on rules which are not known to you. The void around you is not empty. How do you think that much metal got into the gravel you sieved? It is not a void, but an ether instead. It is full of particles, both mystical and natural. How did you not get heavy metal poisoning by now?
    The AED uses sound waves to knock the particles out of the air you breathe. But it is not that easy, every material is acoustically attuned and must be unattuned to use. This serves as the first (potentially optional) automation challenge.
    This is a unique machine in that it skips processing lines but does not scale with energy.
    Automation challenge: every material (hyperbole) has an “acoustic attunement” - a string of 6 possible “directions” which must be zeroed to unattune. Let’s say copper has an attunement of [up], titanium has an attunement of [up, up, down], and tungsten has an attunement of [up, up, down, up, up, down]. If you want to generate a tungsten dust, you would input a catalyst into a bus on the correlating “speaker” in the provided order. But doing so will also generate 4 copper and 2 titanium. Because you need to input the catalysts in a strict order the speed of the machine is limited to 1 tick per attunement length. Max attunement length dictated by plastic tier.
    Acoustic reentanglement mode: Attunes items to specific kind of sound frequencies, unlocking new possibilities. An attuned maintenance hatch increases fuel efficiency of combustion-based generators due to reduced vibration losses. Attuned turbines can spin faster due to reduced vibration.
    Possible tweaks: replace catalyst input with a redstone signal. Generate material attunement randomly on world creation.
