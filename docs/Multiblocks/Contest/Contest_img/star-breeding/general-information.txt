
Lifecycle: 
- There are various types of star seeds
- Star seeds start with 500 stability. each rank up reduces the maximum attainable stability. But it isn't a hard cap so it can keep previous stability values
- Stability decays passively. Higher rank stars will decay faster
- Feeders can add initial mass. Initial mass will then be gradually digested into normal mass
- Harvesters can turn normal mass into byproducts. 
- If both the initial mass and normal mass are 0, The star is considered dead.
- Harvesters will collect the cores of dead stars
- If the base multiblock is unformed, or If the star isn't dead by the time the stability reaches 0, everything in a spherical radius that reaches partway into the multiblocks is deleted 



Rank:
- Stars can't go down in rank
- A star's density is based on the amount of normal mass
- The star's density decides its rank. After ranking up, stability is gained, but stability decay is faster
- To rank up, inital mass needs to equal 0. It can't rank up otherwise
- Stability decays passively depending on how close the density value is to a certain value. This certain value is decided by rank
