With the IK and the PSV, I am suggesting adding a bunch of new food processing lines. These will output full dishes, which will offer massive boosts to bacteria, gcrop, and wetware breeding/growth. This can also be used to extend UV, if you make some of the dishes necessary to breed special bacteria/cells for wetware mainframes. The IK will handle all “kitchen” recipes, whereas the PSV will synthesize all the meat/animal products without ranches. Both multis have parallels, subtick, and batching. When the PSV is running, the inside will render as filled with a classic sci-fi green goop, maybe with some amorphous floating dark blobs.

I would remove all of the random food dishes/intermediates that create, thermal series, and farmer’s delight adds, but keep their crops. I would also make it so that chickens don’t lay eggs, if possible. Keeping breeding animals and milking cows is fine, because it can’t be automated.

Here are some examples of dish processing lines:

PSV: 16 stem cells + 50mB sterilized growth medium + 100mB mutagen + 16 sugar = 64 raw beef
Macerator: 1 wheat = 4 wheat flour
Macerator: 1 onion = 4 diced onions
Macerator: 1 bell pepper = 2 diced bell pepper
Fermenter: 2 wheat flour + 25mB sterilized growth medium = 1 bio chaff + 2000mB yeast
IK: 3 wheat flour + 500mB yeast + 1000mB water = 4 lean dough
IK: 1 lean dough + 1 block of wrought iron (Not Consumed) = 1 flatbread
IK: 1 long wrought iron rod (Not Consumed) + 32 raw beef = 32 doner beef
IK: 1 flatbread + 3 doner beef + 3 diced bell pepper + 5 diced onions = 1 Döner Kebab

1 Döner Kebab can be added to a new recipe for stem cells that triples the output

PSV: 16 stem cells + 150mB sterilized growth medium + 100mB pyrophosphoric acid + 8 sugar = 128 eggs
PSV: 8 stem cells + 100mB sterilized growth medium + 300mB yeast + 16 sugar + 100000mB water = 64000mB milk
Mixer: 1000mB milk = 5 butter
Macerator: 1 cocoa beans = 4 cocoa powder
IK: 2 cocoa powder + 3 sugar + 1500mB seed oil = 2000mB chocolate
IK: 1500mB chocolate + 2 sodium bicarbonate + 5 wheat flour + 10 sugar + 3000mB water + 10 eggs + 500mB seed oil = 4000mB chocolate cake batter
IK: 5 cocoa power + 500mB milk + 16 butter + 16 sugar = 1000mB chocolate buttercream
IK: 500mB chocolate cake batter = 1 chocolate cake base
IK: 3 chocolate cake base + 800mB chocolate buttercream = 1 Triple Layer Chocolate Cake

1 Triple Layer Chocolate Cake can be added to a new recipe for wetware processors that quadruples the output.

Late game, the IK’s recipes will be given as a machine mode to the FARM threading multi, and the PSV’s recipes will be given as a machine mode to the ICR. At high enough voltages, the IK can also do production line skips, producing full dishes from basic ingredients. The PSV can also be used for a bunch of other thematically relevant dragon-related recipes.
