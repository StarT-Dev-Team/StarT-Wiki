**In depth mechanics**: 



The Kamiokande Oscillation Engine has a custom mechanic that propose a new automation challenge.

&#x09;First of all, it has 2 set of 3 recipes, one producing *electron-neutrino*, one for *muon-neutrino*, one for *tau-neutrino* and, optionally 3 more for their antiparticle counterparts with an other machine mode (*electron-antineutrino*, *muon-antineutrino* and *tau-antineutrino*). Each set of recipes consume the same amount of energy and takes the same time 10 seconds to complete.

Firstly, each recipe require the KOE to be at 100% ***Purity***, which can be obtained by consuming x amount of De-Ionized water every 5 seconds (same mechanic as the turbines boost like Lubricant boosted turbines).The ***Purity*** increase by 20% each cycles. He can't run without De-Ionized water, and as soon as you don't provide enough, the ***Purity*** instantly goes to 0% (like a cleanroom when you break a wall for example).

&#x09;Then, there is an ***Efficiency*** amount, ranging from 1%-100% that can be displayed with the Redstone Variadic Interface. It is dependent on the Energy requirement of each recipes and on the output chance, like for example at 1% ***Efficiency***, you consume x100 the energy required for the recipe (i'll explain the energy consumption later) and produce the neutrinos fluxes with 1% chance (not recommended to be honest). Now how to maintain the ***Efficiency*** at a decent level ? Well running the same recipe twice in a row will reduce the ***Efficiency*** by 15% and doing a different recipe will increase the ***Efficiency*** by 30% (max 100% tho) BUT running a different recipe will dump the water inside the KOE resulting in the ***Purity*** going to 0%. 

&#x09;Finally, it works like a mix of a fusion reactor and a vacuum pump : it has an energy buffer and can't run recipes without a full buffer, when the buffer is full, it checks the ***Purity*** (anything but 100% will do nothing) and check the ***Efficiency*** amount. Then it execute the selected recipe with ***Parallels*** depending on the ***Efficiency*** level so that at 1% it execute x1 parallels and at 100% it does x4096 parallels.



Here's some extra useful informations :


The number of ***Parallels*** is defined by : P = f(E) = floor( 0.5 + E^(ln(4096)/ln(100)) )

The energy consumption will always be : Pmax the amount of energy in the KOE buffer (i'll recommend something like x256A UEV)

The minimum energy consumed by a recipe is : Pmax/4096 (only reached at 100% ***Efficiency***)

Doing the same recipe over and over will lead to a 1% ***Efficiency*** KOE which is not optimial (could have guessed it tho)

Doing always a different recipe over and over will lead to the KOE taking most of the time to refill ***Purity*** which is not optimal (bit harder to see but still understandable)

The player would have to find the optimal solution balancing between similar and alternating cycles like "R1 -> R1 -> R1 -> R2 -> R2 -> R2 -> R3 -> R3 -> R3 -> repeat" (i'll call it the triple 3-recipes cycle)
It might be easier to integrate the ***Purity*** mechanic like the temperature of the Hell Forge, running the De-Ionized water recipe but keep in mind that it should always consume x amount of De-Ionized water every 5 seconds even when a recipe is currently processing.

Beware if you want to change some numbers here that might completly change the intented mechanic (like running the same recipe over and over might become the optimal solution).

With theses numbers (especially the 15% penalty, 30% bonus, the 10s recipe duration and the 20% every 5s) makes the triple 3-recipes cycle better than the single recipe, simple 3-recipe cycle and double 3-recipe cycle, but i haven't checked if the quadruple 3-recipes cycle is even better tho.
