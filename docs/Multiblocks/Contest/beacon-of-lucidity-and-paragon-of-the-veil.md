## Summary

Proposed multiblock structures for the **Beacon of Lucidity** and the **Paragon of the Veil**. Both controllers already exist in the pack and already have descriptions, but neither has a multiblock structure yet; this submission proposes one for each. Both are described by the author as a Daydream Spire with infinite connections: the Beacon of Lucidity adds infinite range, and the Paragon of the Veil adds infinite cross-dimensional range.

The Paragon of the Veil was submitted twice: first as a compact build close to the contest size limit, then as a much larger rebuild (67x67x88) that the author posted as the final version together with a `.schem` file.

## Mechanics

- No new mechanics are proposed; both structures are shells for controllers that already exist in-game with their own descriptions.
- **Beacon of Lucidity:** a Daydream Spire with infinite connections and infinite range.
- **Paragon of the Veil:** a Daydream Spire with infinite connections and infinite cross-dimensional range.
- The author notes the large Paragon build is limited by the available building blocks and suggests replacing some blocks with custom casings to make it more expensive. Replacing all the coils with Rhenotax coils would also be possible, but the author does not like that option because it breaks the theme followed by the first three multis.
- In response to a joke about the structure needing upkeep, the author said they like the idea of having to maintain the multiblock to keep it working, "a bit like the KOMARU"; this is not part of the submission.

## Beacon of Lucidity

### Summary

Target tier <UXV>UXV</UXV>. A Daydream Spire with infinite connections and infinite range; the controller already exists in-game and this is the proposed structure for it.

### Structure

A central tower on a cross-shaped base with four smaller side pylons, built from superalloy casings, superconducting coils, europium frames, computer heat vents, and high power casings, with detached floating blocks around the top. The author did not state the dimensions; the block template spans roughly 25x23x25.

## Paragon of the Veil

### Summary

Target tier <OpV>OpV</OpV> (the first version was listed as <UXV>UXV</UXV> or <OpV>OpV</OpV>). A Daydream Spire with infinite connections and infinite cross-dimensional range; the controller already exists in-game and this is the proposed structure for it.

The author first posted a compact version in the same style as the Beacon of Lucidity, then rebuilt it at a far larger scale after asking in the thread whether a KOMARU-sized Paragon would be allowed. The large build is the version the author called final.

### Structure

**Final version:** 67x67x88. A wide, roughly square base of pylons in the same casing-and-coil style as the Beacon of Lucidity, with a tall central crystal tower rising from a large interior hall and a set of interlocking orbital rings around a floating crystal at the very top. The schematic uses superalloy casings, stellarium casings, advanced computer casings, high power casings, computer heat vents, europium frames, superconducting coils, advanced fusion coils, fusion glass, and draco resilient fusion glass. The author said the central crystal is the part they like least and that it would render better with a blue animated casing. The author also said the build was limited by the available building blocks and that some could later be swapped for custom casings.

## Structure

Both structures share one visual theme: light superalloy casings and europium frames, teal superconducting coils, computer heat vents, and high power casings, with loose blocks floating around the upper parts. The Beacon of Lucidity is a single tower with four side pylons; the final Paragon of the Veil is 67x67x88 and dwarfs it, with a crystal spire and orbital rings on top of a pylon field.

## Notes from the thread

- Size limit: the author asked whether a KOMARU-sized Paragon of the Veil would be allowed, since it was far beyond the 17x17x17 constraint. Scarlet suggested building it for testing's sake and posting it in the thread but not as the submission, leaving the final call to the other judges; the author agreed, then later posted the large build as the final Paragon entry with a schematic. Which Paragon version counts as the entry was not settled in the thread.
- On the compact Paragon, Scarlet liked the glass looking like an eye but found the runic casing out of place and suggested shaping the eye on the middle pylon more; the author rebuilt the structure at a larger scale afterwards.
- Balance: hummus argued the structure is cheap with the current blocks and would take about two minutes to craft at its tier, suggesting a casing that does not get absolute parallels and runs at <UXV>UXV</UXV>; Serotonintheft suggested filling the central crystal with expensive blocks. The author agreed and pointed to custom casings as the fix.
- Serotonintheft also suggested the Beacon of Lucidity should either be upsized as much as the Paragon or be given a limited range, since the two are functionally identical for most players, and suggested adding a flat wall of the blue casing to make placing laser hatches easier.
