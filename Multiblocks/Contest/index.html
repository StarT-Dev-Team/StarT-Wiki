    Multiblock Design Contest submissions¶

    In September 2026 the community was invited to design new multiblocks for Star Technology in a Discord forum contest. Every submission is collected here, one page per entry, grouped by the voltage tier the author targeted. The pages reproduce what the authors posted (summary, mechanics, screenshots, schematics) and link back to the original Discord thread; they are not a statement about what will be added to the pack.

    Note

    Entries that span several tiers are listed under their lowest tier. Pages marked incomplete had too little material in the post to describe the design fully.

    ULV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    High Pressure Steam Crucible Furnace (HPSCF) ULV White tea Steam-age multiblock Alloy Smelter that runs the normal recipes at 6x parallel and outputs molten alloy as fluid instead of ingots.
    Steam Centrifuge ULV 54 Steam-powered multiblock running ULV Centrifuge recipes, as an early rubber route and a Create centrifuge alternative.
    Steam Forge ULV Goofert | Woman in training Steam multiblock Alloy Smelter with 8 parallels, meant to give the Alloy Smelter the same steam-multi treatment as the other steam multis.
    Steam Glassworks and Glass Factory Steam and LV to around LuV Shadow Two-structure family: a steam Glassworks replacing the Kiln for glass, and a Glass Factory for colored glass, lenses and possibly crystal chip refining.
    Water Cooled Steam Solidifier (W.C.S.S) ULV Bkyt Steam multiblock Fluid Solidifier (up to Steel) that uses water to cool the molten metal; ingot or liquid-metal input modes.

    LV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    BIO Diesel Refinery (BDR) LV TheSilentOne LV multiblock that turns the raw Seed Oil and Ethanol ingredients directly into Bio-Diesel for Create: Diesel Generators.
    Large Fermenting Casket (LFC) LV Corn Chex Multiblock Fermenting Casket with reduced recipe times and wall sharing that keeps the Bio Chaff output when making Ethanol.
    Mega Mechanical Plate Presser (MMPP) LV TheSkadoodler Late-LV multiblock Bender with 2 parallels that only accepts LV energy hatches (16A allowed); needs an MV circuit to craft.
    Solar Array of Mirrors for Steam and Giant Lumium Overheater of Water (SAMS / GLOW) LV and HV Nuusku Two fuel-free steam boilers: a daytime solar mirror array at LV (~400 mB/t) and an always-on Lumium-powered overheater at HV (~4,000 mB/t).
    Syngas Processing Facility (SPF) (incomplete) LV Uncraftable204 Converts Steam and Coal/Charcoal blocks into Syngas, then processes it into carbon products.
    Waterlogged Mechanical Sieve (WMSv) LV Corn Chex Small LV multiblock that automates the waterlogged sieve; input at the top, output at the bottom.
    Wire Insulation Plant (WIP) LV Kurtchekov LV multiblock that runs only cabling recipes at higher throughput than Assemblers, using Water as a cooling input.

    MV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Large Electric Anemic Fermenter (L.E.A.F.) MV Terralizer MV multiblock fermenter that turns Bone Meal and biomatter into bulk Ethanol for plastic production.
    Low Temperature Pressure Chambers MV, IV, UHV Hash Browns, Serotonintheft Three tiered high-pressure reactors (SPC, BALL, COLOSSUS) that take over key high-pressure Chemical Reactor recipes.

    HV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Bio Reactor HV enlolers HV generator that burns bio fuels such as Bio Chaff with Water to make EU, leaving Ash behind.
    Crude Biodiesel Generator (CBdG) HV Corn Chex GregTech-style multiblock Create diesel engine that burns Crude Biodiesel for power without a coil setup, outputting Dark Ashes.
    Industrial Fermentation Tank (IFT) HV Slime_1001 Multiblock Fermenter with boosted throughput that can also make Bio Chaff via the Fermenting Casket recipe.
    Large Item Vault / Large Fluid Tank IV rosa_Wal_23 IV (or HV) multiblock Super Chest and Super Tank for a single Item or Fluid, with tiered internal casings and a proposed direct ME storage hatch.
    Rotary Kiln HV 𝗡Ξ𝗢 HV-LuV gas-fired kiln for reduction roasting of ores and dusts (plus some Pyrolyse Oven recipes); 4 base parallels, coil speed boosts.
    Sieving Apparatus HV ArcadeGamer62 A multiblock Sieve with boosted throughput that can also run the waterlogged sieve recipes.
    Sieving in Extraordinaire Velocity and Efficiency (S.I.E.V.E.) (incomplete) HV kalaribot, ArcadeGamer62 HV multiblock Sieve with boosted throughput that can also process waterlogged recipes.
    Slurry Leaching Ore Plant (S.L.O.P.) HV Zepher Processes specific ore slurries (30,000 mB per run) into a large primary output plus extra dusts; raw ore slurry excluded.
    Thermal Plant HV enlolers HV generator that burns any fuel with Water to make steam and converts it directly into power.
    Thermic Fluid Heater (TFH) HV Kiiyoko Late-HV multiblock Fluid Heater with no muffler hatch, so the Sterilized Growth Medium chain can be fully multiblocked inside a cleanroom.
    Trace Ore Extractor / Trace Ore Processing Plant (TOE / TOPP) HV (regular) / IV (parallel) novaseer Opposite of the Pulverizer: crushed ore processing that sacrifices primary product for much higher byproduct yield; HV regular and IV parallel versions.
    Wind Turbines/Mills HV enlolers Tall wind turbine tower that generates power from exposure to air or a wind source; static and rotating versions.

    EV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Geothermal Plant (G.P) (incomplete) EV enlolers Tentatively EV generator that uses Lava to boil Water into steam and turns it into power; output left to the devs.
    Lapidary Extraction Unit and Lapidary Extraction Matrix (LEU / LEM) LEU - EV, LEM - IV _Scarf EV and IV multiblock Lapidary Dynamos whose power output scales with the number of unique gemstones supplied.
    Large Assembler (LA) EV Yoyo1309 EV multiblock Assembler with a fixed 4x parallel and coil boosting; no subtick, 4x/16x hatches, or other extras.
    Mass Multi Ore Processing Facility (MMOPF) EV Uncraftable204 EV bulk ore processor; ores must be processed together with similar ores to maximize ore efficiency. Details and pastable in an external Google Doc.
    Thermal Cascade Furnace (TCF) EV Magda Tall EV tower stacking a Smelter, Alloy Smelter and Arc Furnace on three descending coil tiers, with parallels set by the bottom coils.

    IV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Acoustic Entanglement Device (AED) IV Lumen, unknown user Sound-based dust generator fed by catalysts in a strict directional order; skips processing lines but does not scale with energy.
    Catalytic Reaction Chamber (CRC) IV Thomperso Chemical multiblock that runs closed chemical loops (e.g. rutile Titanium line, Formic Acid loop) so only the net input is consumed.
    Joule-driven Unit for Industrial Coating & Electrodeposition (JUICE) IV Serotonintheft Electroplating multiblock that replaces Rhodium-plated Palladium recipes, wearing down a Rhodium anode in a new anode-holder hatch.
    Large Compressor IV joe An IV multiblock Compressor with overclocks, batching, and parallels; Compressor recipes only, no coil overclocking.
    Large Turbine (incomplete) IV kiso2019 A bigger steam turbine for IV-UV that yields more power per steam and can run on Nuclear reactor steam; the existing Large Turbine would become Medium.

    LuV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Atmosphere Dust Collector (A.D.C) LuV enlolers LuV powered collector that yields one tiny pile of each pre-LuV/LuV dust per ~256-second cycle, with no inputs.
    Modular Ingot Processing Facility (MIPF) LuV Wad EBF, ABS and Vacuum Freezer merged into one upgradeable facility with module slots on its Control Station, Smeltery and Chiller.
    Modular Thermal Distillation Plant (MTDP) LuV ArcadeGamer62 LuV distillation plant with up to 6 hot-fluid-driven modules (four tiers) that speed up recipes and cut energy; the main block needs matching coolant.
    Pattern Distribution Machine (PDM) somewhere LuV or ZPM or UV satire Stores AE2 patterns and distributes requested crafts equally across all connected machines of one type over a dedicated cable.
    Stellar Nucleosynthesis Astrochemistry Disassembler (S.N.A.D) LuV rinbow LuV/ZPM machine that turns infused Nether Star shards plus Refinery Gas and dusts into single rare gases (Argon, Neon, Krypton, Xenon, Radon).
    Synergistic Gas Turbine (SGT) LuV Serotonintheft LuV gas turbine that burns up to 5 gases at once; each extra fuel type adds 0.5 to an output multiplier (max 3), at 3 LGTs of consumption per gas.

    ZPM¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Large Cleaving Factory (LCF) ZPM 2treehouse1 Two-mode ZPM multiblock: Laser Engraving plus a new Laser Chip Factory mode turning wafers and a lens directly into engraved chips.
    Layered Universal Circuit Machines ZPM, UHV, UIV Joyal Three tiered bulk circuit assemblers (ZPM/UHV/UIV) replacing the Draco Bulk Circuiter mode, with x24 batched recipes and tier-locked solder.
    Ocean Draining Device (O.D.D) ZPM ArcadeGamer62 Boosted bulk version of the Industrial Fishery that also unlocks seawater recipes such as direct ore and salt extraction.
    Thermal Dissociation Complex (TDC) ZPM venus A Pyrolyse Oven multiblock with parallel hatches for ZPM+ mass processing, styled after the TDA.

    UV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Ancient Belief Yielding Data Optimization Sanctum and Eye of Horus (A.B.Y.D.O.S. and E.O.H.) UV Luck, Dungeon Six Potion, Cry, Martin_mc Two UV Abydos multiblocks: the A.B.Y.D.O.S. turns offered coordinate crystals into data, the E.O.H. laser-drills Abydos through the Classic Stargate.
    Diffracting Resource Inter-Dimensional Lithic Loader (D.R.I.L.L.) UV Luck, Cry, Dungeon Six Potion, Martin_mc UV ore-processing plant that leaches Rock Filtrator and Molten Ore Mixture ores straight to crushed ores and sludges to final dusts.
    Dimensional Resonator (D.R.) UV Luck, Dungeon Six Potion, Cry, Martin_mc Successor to the Dimensional Destabiliser that yields ASG-gated materials early using Gravi-stars; coordinate crystals focus the output per dimension.
    Hawking Radiation Reactor (H.R.R) UV enlolers UV generator that consumes a proposed Darkmatter fuel to create a black hole and harvest its Hawking Radiation for power.
    Industrial Kitchen and Protein Synthesis Vat (IK / PSV) UV Serotonintheft Two UV multiblocks for new food lines: the IK cooks full dishes and the PSV grows meat, eggs, and milk from stem cells, boosting bacteria, gcrop, and wetware.
    Lagrangian Anchored Rift Pulsing Inductive Negating Generator (L.A.R.P.I.N.G.) UV Luck, Dungeon Six Potion, Cry, Martin_mc UV generator that opens a Stargate with Enriched Naquadah Dust and plasmas; output grows over time until upkeep outpaces it.
    ME Wireless Bridge UV White tea Multiblock ME Quantum Link Hub that links ME networks across dimensions; link count scales with controllers inside.
    Mega Epic Omni Wheel (M.E.O.W) UV KampfWolf A giant wheel with a mechanical cat inside; an expensive UV-tier generator that outputs many amps, with better coils proposed as an output multiplier.
    Plasma Factory (PF) UV Slime_1001 UV multiblock that only makes Plasma Turbine plasmas, with reflector overclock, subtick, parallel and laser hatch support plus Helium/Iron/Nickel/Argon fluid bonuses.
    Pyrolytic Compound Breakdown Unit UV デイモス A UV Pyrolyse Oven that distills its own fluid and gas outputs, with Pyrolyse overclock, Parallel Hatch, subtick, and Batch Mode.

    UHV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Chilled IHPCA (CIHPCA) UHV matialox, HARIBO IHPCA variant with Bulk Blast Chiller-style cooling built in; a side rotor holder and rotor scale the cooling and computation.
    Cryothermal Equilibrium Layered Liquidising Synthesiser, FLUID and ITEM (CELLS) UHV Random², Hooded Three linked UHV multiblocks (CELLS, FLUID, ITEM) that assemble the ASG with layered inputs and a heating/cooling mechanic, replacing Mechanical Assemblers.
    Dimensional Runically Engraved Energy Access Medium (DREEAM) UHV ggki Linked pair of UHV laser-hatch multiblocks giving a 1:1, one-way cross-dimensional energy link; linked with a data stick, 1% self-use.
    Dyson Swarm Rail Cannon (DSRC) UHV Dylan the Deltawolfy Solar array with 20 panel slots that consumes Dyson Swarm Drones every 6 s to multiply panel output (up to 8x at UHV) and runs at night.
    Forgotten Phase Shifter (FPS) UHV GenerusWeebius, ggki Late UHV/early UEV multi that changes the phase of input matter via an automation challenge, replacing the CQC's Oganesson BEC and Helium Superfluid recipes.
    Fusion Reactor On Zero Energy Nodes and Crushed Riftic μonic Cold Internal Borealic Engine (FROZEN and CRμCIBLE) UHV and UXV 2treehouse2, 2treehouse1 Two Cold Fusion Reactors (UHV FROZEN, UXV CRμCIBLE) fed with muon-dense akreyrium that output solids and need saturation management.
    Neutron-conserving Atom Destabilizer (NCAD) UHV Lumen, unknown user Rips protons and electrons off pure metal dusts to make Neutronium, with Hydrogen and heavier elements as byproducts.
    Small Modular Nuclear Reactor (SMNR) (incomplete) UHV matialox, Haribo A much smaller late-game nuclear reactor intended to produce more power than a regular one.
    Solar Plasma Extractor (SPEx) UHV TheSkadoodler Abydos-only UHV laser that bombards the sun with photons to produce light plasmas (Hydrogen, Helium) for net-positive power; needs computation.
    Stabilised Artificial Singularity Harvester (S.A.S.H.) UHV Kenobi 17x17x5 ring containing a black hole; one-cycle Implosion/Heat Chamber and UEV/UIV material recipes balanced by exponential containment energy.
    Void Breaking Accelerator (VBA) UHV Serinat Upgradable Void resource/energy generator that is tiered by building new sections (VBA I to III) onto a downward-pointing main chamber.

    UEV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Cyclotron and Ambient Neutron Activator (ANA) UEV JustObsidian A Nether-only ANA that irradiates Dysprosium and a Cyclotron that spends it to transmute fissile materials, skipping fission lines and boosting superheavies.
    Draconic Nuclear Reactor (DNR) UEV Smephy UEV post-draconic bulk Nuclear Reactor that runs the fuel-rod recipes to mass-produce Fermium; no extra mechanics.
    gCrop Breeding-havesting Complex Array (gBhCA) UEV Peaplant Combined EgCHP + gCFA that duplicates a gCrop and grows it into fruit or flower form in one recipe.
    Hyperpolarizing Object Radiation Thermojunction Holder (HORTH) UEV IncurableDressing UEV fission reactor whose parallels scale from 0.25x to 4x with a radiation value built up by throughput; too much radiation voids inputs.
    Kamiokande Oscillation Engine (KOE) UEV Smephy Super-Kamiokande-inspired tank that produces neutrino fluxes for a bulk Neutronium recipe, balancing Purity (De-Ionized Water) against Efficiency.

    UIV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Colossal Amazing Nano Technology Astronomical Legendary Ominous Underworld Puncturing Extractor (C.A.N.T.A.L.O.U.P.E) UIV enlolers Late-game drill that burns Drilling Fluid and a coolant to extract mineral fluids, with the output tier chosen by Circuit.
    Concentrated Overthermal Reaction Engine (C.O.R.E.) UIV easymode A 23x27x23 successor to the heat chambers; optional inner-density mechanic (0-1000 DU) gives up to 4x recipe speed when tuned to each recipe's optimum.
    Condensed Horizon Relativistic Oscillation Nucleus for Output Synthesis (C.H.R.O.N.O.S.) UIV GeneralXHD A one-per-world UIV+ machine that condenses time into Dense Temporal Concentrate, a fluid certain late-game machines consume to alter recipe parameters.
    Draconic Life Breeding Chamber (DLBC) UIV Sonix_790 Egg-shaped late-game chamber that grows Draconic cells on an abyss-influenced medium, paid for with void-fluid atmosphere upkeep, a forced UXV overclock and slow recipes.
    Temporally Insurgent Monocrystal Energizer (TIME) UIV Skylaar Grows advanced crystals by feeding Temporal Accelerants to advance recipe Years while managing Temporal Instability; sync window doubles output.

    UXV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Beacon of Lucidity and Paragon of the Veil UXV (Beacon of Lucidity) / OpV (Paragon of the Veil; first listed as "UXV (or OpV)") Smephy Proposed structures for the existing Beacon of Lucidity (UXV) and Paragon of the Veil (OpV) controllers; the Paragon was rebuilt at 67x67x88.
    Nefarious Extradimensional Temporally Hyper Energetic Roaster (N.E.T.H.E.R) (incomplete) UXV Dr. Hans UXV octahedron for all heat-based recipes; energy, speed, and parallels trade off against each other.
    Star Breeding UXV White tea Seven UXV modules around a Stellar Breeding Base that seed, feed, stabilize, harvest, kill, and draw power from living stars with a shared stability/mass lifecycle.

    OpV¶

    Multiblock Tier Author(s) Summary
    Draconic Reactor for Accelerated Generation of Ouroboric Nanofusion (DRAGoN) OpV Kiiyoko Post-DSG ouroboros-dragon Fusion Reactor with absolute parallel hatch, threading controller, 13 helices, and laser inputs instead of energy hatches.
